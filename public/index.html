DATASPHERE
Evidence event plane

The record of what happened.

Datasphere is the append-only event and provenance plane connecting independently governed systems. It records observations and outcomes without inventing authority, trust, or certainty.

Immutable streams

Events are hash-linked per stream. Corrections append new facts instead of rewriting history.

Tenant sovereignty

Database-enforced row-level security isolates every tenant and every machine event.

Signed M2M intake

Short-lived, audience-bound machine envelopes are verified and replay-protected before persistence.
